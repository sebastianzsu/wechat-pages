使用指南 · Opened right inside WeChat ✓

How to ask · 怎么问

  • Direct chat: just send a message.
  • Group chat: @mention the account, then ask.
  • 群里请先 @ 我，再提问。

Commands · 指令

/newStart a fresh conversation · 重新开始对话
/remember …Save a note for this chat · 记住一件事
/memoryShow saved notes · 查看记住的内容
/forgetClear saved notes · 清除记忆
/modelShow which model this chat uses · 查看当前模型

Files & pages · 文件和网页

  • Ask for a web page: “make me a page with …”. It arrives as a link you tap to open.
  • 要网页就说“做一个网页……”，点链接就能打开。
  • Files: txt, csv, md, svg, ics (calendar). Not yet: PDF, Word, photos.
  • Pages are public to anyone with the link. 网页任何拿到链接的人都能看。
